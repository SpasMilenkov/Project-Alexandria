<Aside type="caution" title="Pre-alpha">
  Alexandria is pre-alpha software built for a home network. Expect breaking changes, and keep
  your own copies of anything you store in it.
</Aside>

## What it does

<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="Files with history" icon="document">
    Upload, organise and version files. Previews are generated in the background for images,
    documents, audio, video, archives and text, and deleted items wait in the trash.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Streaming built in" icon="seti:audio">
    Music and video with playlists, synced lyrics, transcoding and listening recaps, served from
    your own machine.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Notes and documents" icon="pencil">
    Write linked notes that embed your files, and search inside the text of your documents.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Automation" icon="random">
    Folder policies, auto-tagging and background jobs keep the library organised without manual
    work.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Optional encryption" icon="seti:lock">
    Encrypt individual files in your browser at upload with a password of your own, so the
    server only ever stores ciphertext.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Monitoring" icon="information">
    Service health, incidents and an activity log for admins, plus a public status page.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
